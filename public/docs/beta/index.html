1Quick Start — Login & Account Setup

1.1 Log in

  1. Open https://sms-beta.web.app
  2. Click Login (top-right corner)
  3. Enter the email and password issued to you by the AviaSAFE administrator

After login you are redirected to your role-based dashboard:

RoleWhat you see
AIRLINE_ADMINYour airline's tenant dashboard: SMS Maturity score, operational risk overview, recent reports, survey status, hazard register, CAN/CAP
CAAN_SMDCAAN SSP Dashboard: cross-tenant aggregated metrics across all operators (read + confirm risk assessments)
USERSafety reporting interface: submit and view VSR / MOR for your airline

1.2 Change your password

Your provisioned password is shared for the beta and must be changed on first login. Use the Forgot Password flow on the login page to set a personal password (16+ characters recommended).

Security: never share your password. The platform never stores or documents passwords in plaintext. Report suspected credential exposure to info@aviasafesystems.com.

1.3 Confirm your scope

  • AIRLINE_ADMIN sees only your airline's tenant name and data.
  • CAAN_SMD sees the aggregated / state view.
  • USER sees only your airline's reporting surfaces and cannot confirm risk assessments (the control is absent or returns 403 — expected).

If your role or tenant looks wrong after sign-in, sign out and back in; if it persists, contact your administrator rather than changing it yourself.

2Beta Test Checklist

Access: https://sms-beta.web.app — isolated beta environment; all writes go to sms-db-beta.

2.1 Authentication & Basic Access

  • Navigate to the beta URL and log in with your provided credentials
  • Verify role-based access (Airline Admin vs CAAN_SMD)
  • Confirm the [BETA NOTICE] banner appears on the survey and CAAN State Risk pages

2.2 Voluntary Safety Reporting (VSR)

  • Submit a new VSR with valid data
  • Verify the report appears in the list view, tagged with your tenant
  • Toggle Report anonymously on one VSR and confirm it is accepted

2.3 Hazard Registration & Risk Matrix

  • Create a hazard from a VSR
  • Verify risk-matrix calculation: S3 × P3 = 9 → Medium / Tolerable and S2 × P2 = 4 → Low / Acceptable
  • Confirm status transitions (Under Review → Active → Closed)

2.4 Corrective Action Plans (CAP)

  • Create a CAP for an active hazard
  • Assign responsibility to a user
  • Update CAP status (Draft → In Progress → Complete) and verify the audit/history trail inside the CAN/CAP document

2.5 Rate Limiting

  • Confirm sustained activity on a protected endpoint returns 429 with X-RateLimit-* headers
  • Confirm limits reset hourly and a different IP can still log in during a limited window (limits are per-IP)

2.6 Cross-Tenant Isolation (CAAN_SMD only)

  • Log in as a CAAN_SMD user and view aggregated data across tenants
  • Confirm you have no write access to individual airline data

2.7 Expected Behavior

  • All API calls route to the beta backend; all database writes land in sms-db-beta
  • Point-in-Time Recovery (PITR) is enabled on sms-db-beta
  • No production data is affected by any beta action

3Hazard & Reporting Taxonomy Reference

Risk levels: the canonical classification is Low | Medium | High | Very High, derived from the ICAO 5×5 matrix (risk_index = severity × probability). Default thresholds: risk index ≤ 5 → Low; ≤ 9 → Medium; ≤ 15 → High; else → Very High.

Occurrence Types

Runway Excursion · Runway Incursion · Airborne Conflict · Abnormal Runway Contact · Ground Collision · System/Component Failure · Powerplant Failure · Weather Encounter · Bird Strike · Cabin Safety Event · Procedural Deviation · ATC Operational Incident

Human Factors

Risk Levels

Low Medium High Very High

Phases of Flight
