# Account & Profile Setup

**Step:** 02 — Account & Profile Setup  
**Guide:** AviaSAFE Tenant Onboarding Guide  
**Applies to:** AIRLINE_ADMIN, CAAN_SMD, USER  
**Prerequisites:** Step 01 (Getting Started) — an account has been provisioned for you by your
administrator

---

## 1. Overview

### 1.1 Purpose
Confirm that your account has the correct identity, role, and tenant scope before you begin using
the platform. This step covers verifying your role, setting a strong personal password, and
understanding what your role can and cannot do.

### 1.2 Business Context
AviaSAFE authorizes users through **Firebase custom claims** — each user is assigned a `role`
(`USER`, `AIRLINE_ADMIN`, `CAAN_SMD`, or `SUPER_ADMIN`) and a `tenant_id` (which airline they
belong to). Claims can take a few seconds to propagate after an account is created; the platform
has a built-in fallback that looks up your tenant by email during that window.

Role determines scope:
- `AIRLINE_ADMIN` — full management of your airline's tenant (reports, hazards, CAN/CAP, risk
  matrix config, confirming risk assessments).
- `USER` — submit and view reports for your airline only; cannot confirm risk assessments.
- `CAAN_SMD` — cross-tenant read + confirm risk assessments (State / SSP inspectorate).

### 1.3 Expected Outcome
- A verified account with the correct role and tenant scope.
- A strong, personal password (the initial provisioned password is shared/env-configured and must
  be changed).
- Knowledge of your role's access boundaries.

## 2. Step-by-Step UI Guide

### 2.1 Navigation
1. Log in at **[https://sms.aviasafesystems.com](https://sms.aviasafesystems.com)** (Step 01).
2. If you were provisioned with a temporary password, change it in your Google/Firebase account
   settings (or use the platform's "Forgot Password" flow to set a personal password).

### 2.2 Input Matrix

| # | Field / Action | Type | Required | Constraints / Format | Notes |
|---|---------------|------|----------|---------------------|-------|
| 1 | Current password | password | Yes | — | Temporary provisioned password |
| 2 | New password | password | Yes | Min 8 characters; use 16+ for security | Strong password policy recommended |
| 3 | Role / tenant verification | action | — | — | Not editable by the user — assigned by your administrator |

### 2.3 Execution
1. Sign in with your provisioned credentials.
2. Change your password to a personal, strong password (see the security guidance in
   [Security](#security-notes)).
3. Navigate to the dashboard area relevant to your role and confirm the data you see matches your
   airline's tenant (e.g., AIRLINE_ADMIN sees your airline name; CAAN_SMD sees the cross-tenant /
   state view).
4. If your role or tenant looks wrong, contact your administrator rather than attempting to change
   it yourself.

### 2.4 Success Confirmation
- You are signed in and your dashboard shows the correct tenant scope.
- You can sign out and back in with your new personal password.
- AIRLINE_ADMIN sees your tenant name; CAAN_SMD sees aggregated/cross-tenant data; USER sees only
  your airline's reporting surfaces.

## 3. Edge Cases

| Scenario | Expected Behaviour | Notes |
|----------|-------------------|-------|
| Role claims not applied immediately after provisioning | Dashboard may temporarily show limited data | Re-login; the email→tenant fallback covers most cases; contact administrator if it persists |
| Provisioned password unknown | Use the platform's "Forgot Password" reset flow | Your administrator can also reset via Firebase console |
| Wrong tenant shown after sign-in | Tenant derived from email domain / claims | Contact your administrator (tenant-id drift is reconciled by `/fix-tenant-ids`) |
| USER tries to confirm a risk assessment | Button is absent or request rejected (403) | Expected — only AIRLINE_ADMIN / CAAN_SMD / SUPER_ADMIN may confirm |

### Common Errors

| Error Message | Cause | Resolution |
|--------------|-------|------------|
| "Failed to fetch user data." | Claims not yet propagated or network issue | Sign out and sign in again |
| "403 Forbidden" on an admin action | Authenticated as USER, not AIRLINE_ADMIN | Request the correct role from your administrator |

---

## 4. Security Notes

- Never share your password. The platform never documents passwords in plaintext and expects the
  same from its users.
- Change the shared provisioned password on first login.
- Report suspected credential exposure to your administrator immediately.

## 5. QA Checklist

- [ ] User can sign in with provisioned credentials

- [ ] Role-based scoping correct for AIRLINE_ADMIN (own tenant), CAAN_SMD (cross-tenant), USER (own tenant submit/read)
- [ ] Confirming risk assessments works for AIRLINE_ADMIN / CAAN_SMD but is rejected for USER
- [ ] Wrong-tenant or wrong-role issues escalate to administrator correctly
- [ ] No plaintext password appears anywhere in the UI or documentation
