ICAO Annex 19 · Doc 9859 · Doc 10951 Aligned

From Safety Reporting to Safety Intelligence

Built for the next evolution of aviation safety management, AviaSAFE SMS is founded on ICAO Annex 19, Doc 9859 (Safety Management Manual) and Doc 10951 (Safety Intelligence Manual). It helps organizations measure SMS maturity, identify hazards, assess and mitigate risks, and turn safety data into safety intelligence — understanding risks before they become events.
