Executive Summary

Over 45 years of executive and operational leadership in the aviation industry. Former CEO of a domestic airline in Nepal, Human Factors Trainer/Instructor, and Advisor to AWS Education. Dedicated to embedding proactive safety cultures across High Reliability Organizations (HROs) and promoting AI education to enhance aviation safety performance and accelerate learning cycles.

Core Expertise & Specializations

  • Human Factors Training & Curriculum Design: Aligned with ICAO Doc 9683, NCAR Part-66 Module 9, Crew Resource Management (CRM), and Fatigue Risk Management (FRMS).
  • Incident & Accident Investigation: Applied practitioner competence in HFACS, SHELL Model, Reason's Swiss Cheese, and Bow-Tie investigation methodologies.
  • Safety Management Systems (SMS) & Annex 19: End-to-end SMS design, 4-Pillar Safety Culture surveys, and continuous performance monitoring.
  • HRO Principles & AI Education: Translating operational data into actionable safety intelligence and advocating AI education to minimize safety learning curves.
