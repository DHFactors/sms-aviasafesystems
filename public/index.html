Foundational Architecture

From Compliance to Safety Intelligence

The objective of a modern Safety Management System is not merely collecting safety data. ICAO Annex 19 (3rd Edition), the Safety Management Manual (Doc 9859), and the Safety Intelligence Manual (Doc 10159) emphasize the importance of transforming safety information into meaningful safety action.

Core Principle

"Collecting safety data is not the goal.
Turning safety data into safer decisions is."

Modern SMS programs generate large volumes of occurrence reports, audit findings, hazards, inspections, investigations, and safety performance data. The challenge is connecting that information, identifying meaningful patterns, understanding emerging risks, and ensuring management can act before incidents occur.

AviaSAFE SMS helps organizations move beyond data collection by providing a single environment for safety reporting, risk management, assurance activities, performance monitoring, and safety intelligence.

Regulatory & Compliance Alignment

  • ICAO Annex 19 Aligned: Supports full implementation of key Safety Management and State Safety Programme (SSP) frameworks.
  • State Safety Programme Ready: Purpose-built for civil aviation authority oversight, operator audits, and national safety objectives.
  • Safety Risk Management: Structured hazard identification, 5x5 SRA matrix, mitigation tracking, and continuous monitoring.
  • Safety Assurance Enabled: Integrated performance monitoring, audits, inspections, investigations, and CAP verification.

Enterprise Platform Security

  • Secure Cloud Architecture: Enterprise-grade infrastructure, strict tenant isolation, and SOC2-ready security controls.
  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Granular permissions tailored to regulators, safety managers, postholders, and staff.
  • Encrypted Data Protection: End-to-end encryption in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256) for all confidential reports.
